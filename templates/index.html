My research seeks to enable networked systems to support the next generation of applications. My past work has spanned internet congestion control, video streaming, privacy-preserving computation, wireless networks, and mobile systems. A common theme across these is the use of theoretical ideas to gain insights into the real-world that would have been difficult to discover otherwise. I co-lead the UT Networked Systems Lab (UTNS).

Teaching

CS 395T: Performance Analysis of Networked Systems

A seminar course that surveys the role that theoretical performance analysis has had in informing the design of real-world systems. Ideally, we want our theory to give us practical insights that go beyond what can be obtained via intuition or empirical analysis alone.

Years: Spring 2024, Spring 2025, Spring 2026

CS 356: Computer Networks

A standard undergraduate course in networking with a focus on how the internet works today.

Years: Fall 2024, Fall 2025
